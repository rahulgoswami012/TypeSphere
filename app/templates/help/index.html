1. Speed, Precision & Cadence Mathematics

Net WPM (Words Per Minute)

The official velocity standard: 1 Word = Exactly 5 Keystrokes (including spaces). Net WPM accounts strictly for verified correct strokes over active minutes elapsed:
Net WPM = (Correct Characters / 5) / Elapsed Minutes

Accuracy & Total Mistakes

Accuracy represents the percentage of correct strikes versus total keys pressed. TypeSphere tracks both Cumulative Keystroke Mistakes (all mistypes made) and Uncorrected Errors remaining at run conclusion.

Rhythm Consistency

Measures interval variance between consecutive keystrokes. Higher consistency (85%+) indicates smooth, uninterrupted muscle memory without cognitive hesitation stalls.

2. Flight Modes: Blind, Ghost & 5-Shield Survival

Blind Flight Mode (Default: ON): Restricts backspace corrections to train forward velocity and cognitive flow. Typists learn not to stutter on errors, developing immediate kinetic rhythm.
Ghost Pacer Competitor (Default: OFF): A dynamic luminous pink pacer that glides across the text at your target pace, allowing you to visually pace yourself against your personal best or rivals.
5-Shield Survival Protocol: The ultimate stress benchmark. You begin with 5 Cockpit Battery Cells (100% Shield Integrity). Each typo causes a battery overload. Exhausting all 5 cells triggers a hull breach and immediate flight termination.

5. Arcade Flight Disciplines & Mechanics

Launch Arcade Flight Modules →

Each educational arcade module isolates a distinct biomechanical reflex — from peripheral vision and single-key precision to latency reaction:

🏎️ Speed Racer

Discipline: Momentum & Recoil Sprint.
Correct strokes accelerate your vehicle; typos trigger backward recoil without locking the input. Beat the pace bot across the finish line.

🫧 Bubble Pop

Discipline: Single-Key Tactile Precision.
Floating bubbles contain isolated target characters. Pop targets before they collide with the ceiling line within the active countdown.

🔨 Whack-A-Word

Discipline: Spatial Reaction Latency.
Targets pop up in a 3x3 reaction grid with shrinking exposure windows. Measures millisecond reaction speed before targets retract.

⚔️ Zombie Duel

Discipline: Head-to-Head 100 HP Combat.
Rapid typing casts attack projectiles against your rival; typos trigger defensive shield overload and recoil damage.

💻 Cipher Hacker

Discipline: Layered Cryptographic Breach.
Decrypt progressive security tiers (hexadecimal dumps, authentication tokens, system commands) before the firewall countdown expires.

☄️ Falling Words / Space Defender

Discipline: Multi-Wave Orbital Defense.
Lock onto descending phrases and incoming space debris across 5 successive waves before they breach defensive barriers.

💣 Bomb Defuse

Discipline: Sequential Pressure Cracking.
Crack sequential multi-character cryptograms against a strict 40-second digital countdown before detonation.

🧠 Memory Type

Discipline: Working Cognitive Recall.
Sequences flash for 1.5 seconds then mask. Reproduce the target sequence strictly from mental recall across rounds.

🗺️ Keyboard Quest

Discipline: Ergonomic Keyboard Progression.
Train progressive finger reaches: Home Row resting anchors, upper row reaches, bottom row tucks, and top number stretches.
